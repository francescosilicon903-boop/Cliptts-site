Why it exists

I looked for it for weeks: a local voice reader, with a voice natural enough not to kill my urge to write. I never found it — so I built it.

The starting point was simple: the clipboard is already the connection Windows offers between every program. ClipTTS uses it as a universal entry point — it's not another window to open, it's the one that follows you wherever you already are.

How it works

01

Copy a text

From Obsidian, your browser, an editor, anywhere on your PC.

02

ClipTTS notices on its own

It comes to the front automatically, ready. Or stays in the Tray Icon: press Play from there. No shortcut to remember.

03

Press play

Ten voices, thirty-two languages, on-the-fly pronunciation correction. Starts in under 3 seconds, regardless of text length — CPU only, no GPU required. All local.
